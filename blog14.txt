# What Is a Yellow Card in Football? Rules Explained

Yellow cards are one of the most recognizable parts of football. When a referee shows a yellow card, it means a player has been officially cautioned for an offense.

But what does a yellow card actually mean, and what happens when a player receives one?

## What Is a Yellow Card?

A yellow card is a formal warning given by the referee to a player, substitute, substituted player, or team official for certain offenses.

The referee records the caution as part of the match.

A yellow card does not automatically mean that the player has to leave the field.

## Why Can a Player Receive a Yellow Card?

There are several reasons why a referee may issue a yellow card.

Examples include:

* Unsporting behavior
* Delaying the restart of play
* Showing dissent toward a referee's decision
* Failing to respect the required distance during a restart
* Repeatedly committing fouls
* Entering or leaving the field without permission
* Certain celebrations or other forms of misconduct

The exact decision depends on the circumstances and the Laws of the Game.

## What Happens After a Yellow Card?

The player can normally continue playing after receiving a yellow card.

However, the caution remains on their record for the match.

This means players who have already been booked often have to be careful about committing another offense.

## What Happens If a Player Gets Two Yellow Cards?

If a player receives **two yellow cards in the same match**, the referee shows the red card after the second caution.

The player must then leave the field and cannot continue participating in the match.

Their team must continue with fewer players.

## Yellow Cards in Different Competitions

The consequences of yellow cards beyond an individual match can vary depending on the competition.

For example, some competitions have rules involving accumulated yellow cards and suspensions after a certain number of cautions.

These rules are set by the competition or governing organization.

## Yellow Cards and Substitutes

Yellow cards are not limited to players who are currently on the pitch.

Substitutes and team officials can also receive disciplinary sanctions for certain offenses.

This is particularly important because misconduct can occur from the technical area as well as on the field.

## Why Do Yellow Cards Matter?

A yellow card can affect how a player approaches the rest of a match.

A defender who has already been booked may need to be especially careful when making challenges. A second caution could result in their dismissal.

This can also influence tactical decisions, including substitutions.

## Yellow Card vs. Red Card

The two cards have very different immediate consequences.

**Yellow card:** A caution. The player can normally continue playing.

**Red card:** The player is sent off and cannot continue playing.

Two yellow cards shown to the same player during one match also result in a red card.

## Final Thoughts

Yellow cards help referees manage player behavior and enforce the rules of football.

A single yellow card does not remove a player from the match, but it can have important consequences if another caution follows. Understanding the card system makes disciplinary decisions during football matches much easier to follow.
