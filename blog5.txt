# How Long Is a Football Match? Understanding Match Time

If you are new to football, one of the first things you may wonder is how long a match actually lasts. A standard football match has a straightforward structure, but the total time can be longer than the basic 90 minutes shown on the schedule.

## How Long Is a Standard Football Match?

A standard football match consists of **two 45-minute halves**, making 90 minutes of regulation playing time.

There is a halftime interval between the two halves. The halftime break is normally no longer than 15 minutes.

However, the actual time from kickoff until the final whistle is usually longer than 90 minutes.

## What Is Added Time?

During a match, play can be temporarily stopped for things such as injuries, substitutions, time-wasting, goal celebrations, and other interruptions.

The referee adds time at the end of each half to account for certain periods when play was stopped.

This is commonly called **added time**, **stoppage time**, or **additional time**.

For example, if the referee announces five minutes of added time at the end of the second half, the match continues beyond the original 90-minute mark.

The referee determines when that additional period ends.

## Is Added Time Always the Same?

No. The amount of added time can vary from match to match.

A match with several injuries, substitutions, lengthy stoppages, or video reviews can have more added time than a match with very few interruptions.

The fourth official usually displays the minimum amount of added time at the end of each half.

## What Happens If a Match Is Tied?

In many league matches, a draw is an acceptable result. The match ends after regulation time and any applicable added time.

Knockout competitions can be different.

If a competition requires one team to advance, its rules may provide for **extra time** if the score is level after regulation.

## How Long Is Extra Time?

Extra time consists of two additional periods of **15 minutes**.

That means a team can play up to another 30 minutes before a winner is determined.

There can also be added time at the end of each period of extra time.

If the score remains level after extra time, the competition's rules may use a penalty shootout to determine which team advances.

## What Is a Penalty Shootout?

A penalty shootout is a method used to determine a winner after a knockout match remains tied through the required periods of play.

Players take kicks from the penalty mark according to the competition's procedures.

A shootout is separate from the match's normal playing time and does not count as additional match minutes in the same way as regulation or extra time.

## Why Does the Clock Keep Running?

Football uses a continuous clock rather than stopping the clock every time the ball goes out of play.

The referee keeps track of certain interruptions and adds appropriate time at the end of the half.

This is different from sports where the game clock may stop frequently during play.

## How Long Does a Football Match Usually Take?

A normal match includes:

* 45 minutes for the first half
* Halftime
* 45 minutes for the second half
* Added time in both halves

As a result, watching a complete match usually takes considerably longer than 90 minutes.

Matches that go to extra time take longer still, and a penalty shootout can extend the event further.

## Final Thoughts

The basic structure of football is simple: two 45-minute halves separated by halftime.

However, added time means the final whistle does not necessarily come exactly 90 minutes after kickoff. In knockout competitions, extra time and potentially a penalty shootout can make the match considerably longer.

Understanding these different stages makes it much easier to follow the timing of football matches.
