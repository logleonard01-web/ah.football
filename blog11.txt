# What Is a Corner Kick in Football? Rules Explained

A corner kick is one of the most common attacking situations in football. It gives a team an opportunity to send the ball into the opponent's penalty area and create a scoring chance.

But when is a corner awarded, and what are the rules surrounding it?

## What Is a Corner Kick?

A corner kick is awarded when the ball completely crosses the goal line without a goal being scored, and the last player to touch the ball was a defending player.

The attacking team then gets to restart play from the corner area closest to where the ball went out.

## Where Is the Corner Kick Taken?

The ball is placed inside the **corner area**, which is marked by a quarter-circle at each corner of the pitch.

The ball must be stationary before the kick is taken.

The player taking the corner can kick the ball directly toward the penalty area or pass it to a teammate.

## Can You Score Directly From a Corner?

Yes.

A player can score directly from a corner kick into the opponent's goal.

This is sometimes called an **Olympic goal** or **Olympico**, although the term is more commonly used when the ball curls directly into the goal from the corner.

However, a player cannot score an own goal directly from a corner kick. If the ball goes directly into the kicker's own goal, the restart is a corner kick for the opposing team.

## What Do Teams Do During a Corner?

Corners can involve carefully planned tactics.

Attacking players may position themselves:

* Near the goalkeeper
* Around the penalty spot
* At the far post
* Near the edge of the penalty area

They may then make different runs when the ball is kicked.

Defending teams can use different approaches too. Some teams use **zonal marking**, while others assign defenders to specific opponents.

## Short Corners

Not every corner is crossed directly into the penalty area.

A team can take a **short corner**, where the kicker passes to a nearby teammate.

This can allow the attacking team to move the defense around and create a different angle for a cross or shot.

## Can a Corner Kick Be Taken Quickly?

Yes.

A corner does not necessarily require the defending team to organize itself before the kick is taken.

Teams can use quick corners to restart play before the opposition is fully prepared.

## Corner Kicks and Goals

Corners are an important source of attacking opportunities because the ball is deliberately delivered into a dangerous area of the pitch.

A successful corner can lead to:

* A header
* A volley
* A shot from outside the box
* A rebound
* A second attacking phase

Some teams spend significant time practicing corner routines because set pieces can play an important role in matches.

## Corner Kick vs. Goal Kick

A corner kick and goal kick are both awarded when the ball crosses the goal line, but the decision depends on who touched the ball last.

**Corner kick:** The defending team touched the ball last.

**Goal kick:** The attacking team touched the ball last.

This simple distinction helps explain many decisions near the goal.

## Final Thoughts

Corner kicks give attacking teams a valuable chance to create opportunities from a controlled restart. They can be crossed into the box, played short, or even taken directly toward goal.

Once you understand when a corner is awarded and how teams use them, you'll start noticing the different tactics involved during matches.
