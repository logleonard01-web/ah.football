# Understanding Football Positions: What Does Each Player Do?

Football is easier to understand when you know what each player is expected to do. Although players can move around the pitch during a match, their starting positions usually determine their main responsibilities.

From goalkeepers to forwards, every position contributes differently to the team's overall strategy.

## Goalkeeper

The goalkeeper is the last line of defense. Their primary job is to prevent the opposing team from scoring.

Goalkeepers can use their hands inside their own penalty area, unlike outfield players. They also need to communicate with defenders, organize the defense, collect crosses, and start attacks with throws or kicks.

Modern goalkeepers are often involved in passing as well, especially when their team wants to build an attack from the back.

## Defenders

Defenders generally play closer to their own goal and focus on stopping opposing attacks.

### Center-Backs

Center-backs usually operate in the middle of the defense. They may be responsible for marking opposing forwards, winning aerial challenges, blocking shots, and intercepting passes.

They can also contribute when their team has the ball by passing into midfield or starting attacks.

### Full-Backs

Full-backs normally play toward the sides of the defense. They have both defensive and attacking responsibilities.

When defending, they may track opposing wingers and prevent crosses. When attacking, they can move forward to provide width and deliver crosses into the penalty area.
